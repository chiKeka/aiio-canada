## Ontario, British Columbia and Quebec publish incompatible power-planning quantities

- **Item ID:** `PROVINCIAL_POWER_PLANNING_CONTRACT_2026_09`
- **Evidence status:** `observed`
- **Geography:** `PR_35, PR_59, PR_24`
- **Sources:** [IESO_APO_2026_DATA_TABLES](https://ieso.ca/-/media/Files/IESO/Document-Library/planning-forecasts/apo/2026/2026-Annual-Planning-Outlook-Data-Tables.xlsx), [IESO_APO_2026_DEMAND_MODULE](https://www.ieso.ca/-/media/Files/IESO/Document-Library/planning-forecasts/apo/2026/2026-APO-Demand-Forecast-Module.pdf), [BC_HYDRO_IRP_2025_SUMMARY](https://www.bchydro.com/toolbar/about/strategies-plans-regulatory/supply-operations/long-term-electricity-planning/integrated-resource-plan.html), [QUEBEC_SUPPLY_PLAN_PROGRESS_2025](https://www.regie-energie.qc.ca/storage/app/media/Suivis/Suivi%20D-2023-109/etat-davancement-2025.pdf), [HYDRO_QUEBEC_DATA_CENTRE_RATE_2026](https://news.hydroquebec.com/news/press-releases/all-quebec/hydro-quebec-proposing-regie-energie-new-rate-large-data-centres-adjustment-rate-cryptographic-use-applied-blockchains.html)

**Observed or inferred change:** Ontario's reference scenario publishes data-centre annual energy rising from 4.5 TWh in 2027 to 22.6 TWh in 2050. Quebec's supply-plan filing publishes data-centre winter peak rising from 297 MW in 2025-26 to 1,102 MW in 2034-35. B.C.'s public plan summary identifies supply and transmission actions but does not publish a directly comparable data-centre demand path.

**Potential model implication:** Preserve energy, peak-demand and plan-action quantities in separate fields. Do not pool Ontario TWh with Quebec MW, treat B.C. supply actions as spare capacity, or derive a transmission build requirement from province-wide AI capital expenditure.

**Model action:** `review_candidate` — no automatic parameter or baseline change.

## Provincial public-building price histories do not pass the locked forecast gate

- **Item ID:** `PROVINCIAL_BCPI_REFERENCE_GATE_2026_09`
- **Evidence status:** `inferred`
- **Geography:** `PR_59, PR_35, PR_24`
- **Sources:** [STATCAN_BCPI_18100289](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1810028901)

**Observed or inferred change:** The first British Columbia, Ontario and Quebec reference-class run preserves four public-building composites per province and 38 quarterly observations per series from 2017 Q1 through 2026 Q2. Under the predeclared Alberta validation protocol, all 60 province, asset and one- through five-year horizon checks are not assessed because the required calibration and validation origin counts are unavailable.

**Potential model implication:** Keep every provincial reference projection null and Decision Mode unavailable outside Alberta. Do not transfer Calgary or Edmonton coefficients to another province or relax the protocol after seeing the result.

**Model action:** `no_change` — no automatic parameter or baseline change.
