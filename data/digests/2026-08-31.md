## Alberta non-residential building cost indexes rose in 2026 Q2

**Evidence status:** `observed`  
**Source:** [STATCAN_BCPI_18100289](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1810028901)

**Observed change:** The non-residential building composite rose from 110.1 to 111.9 in Calgary and from 111.5 to 113.1 in Edmonton between 2026 Q1 and Q2 (2023=100). These are approximately 1.6% and 1.4% quarter-over-quarter increases, respectively; the series is not seasonally adjusted and can be revised.

**Potential model implication:** Update the Alberta construction-cost baseline after validating component-level movements. Do not attribute the change to AI investment; it is a pre-scenario market observation.

## AESO's 2026 outlook design treats data-centre growth as a major uncertainty

**Evidence status:** `observed`  
**Source:** [AESO_LTO_2026_INPUTS](https://aesoengage.aeso.ca/2026-long-term-outlook-plan)

**Observed change:** AESO's 2026 Long-Term Outlook engagement material explicitly identifies increased data-centre development interest as a source of uncertainty for load growth and generation needs through 2050. The final outlook has not yet been published on this page.

**Potential model implication:** Keep multiple load-realization pathways and connection-stage probabilities in the Alberta power module rather than using a single data-centre load forecast.
